## Promotional text

Meet fuzzy, smiling Fonsters in a little world of play, dance and care. Make up a
name, grow familiar rituals, and explore together. No Fonsters signup needed.

## Description

Fonsters are fuzzy little friends with a world to explore.

Walk around a landscape lobby on iPhone, find a friend and tap to spend time
together in portrait. Wave, play, nap, dance, stroke their face or give a high five.
Spotlights, a DJ, a mirror ball and optional celebrations turn the same world into
a dance floor.

Make up a name and story for your Fonster. Familiar play gently shapes its
reactions; there are no care penalties for time away. Practice a wave or rhythm
with a grown-up and choose whether to keep it. Undo and Reset are available.

Optional camera mirroring and short English spoken actions run on your device,
with a grown-up task and your device permissions. On-device speech support is
required. Action icons always work. Compatible Apple Intelligence devices can
interpret short typed physical requests locally.

Protected play has no Fonsters signup, ads, analytics SDKs, public feeds, direct
messaging, stranger matchmaking or external agent control. The lobby companions
are local creatures and imported snapshots. Your creature library is saved on
device and can use the existing private iCloud setup; care/movement memories are
local. Actual cross-device syncing of new profile fields needs release validation.

A grown-up can share a snapshot or use the original pixel portrait exports and
links. Feelings and backstory are optional in visit files. Recipients can keep a
copy. Original seed links contain recoverable seed text, so review them before
sharing. The original 2D appearance remains deterministic and exports PNG/GIF,
with JPEG on Mac.

Use Still mode or Reduce Motion for steady poses and lights. Motion pauses with
the app in the background or Low Power Mode. Camera and microphone start off.
Read Privacy & family from the hand icon at any time.

## Review notes

- Protected play is the current experience for all visitors. There is no age
  claim, account signup or social-service unlock in this build.
- Parent tasks guard sharing, original export galleries, Messages stickers and
  enabling device inputs. They are not age assurance or verifiable consent.
- Remote flag/third-party quote requests are disabled at their entry points;
  random text and feature defaults are local. No advertising/tracking SDKs.
- Camera and Speech use local processing and do not retain raw input. No cloud
  speech fallback. The model interprets actions only; there is no AI conversation.
- Existing private iCloud library sync remains; local care files are distinct.
- iPhone: lobby landscape in both directions, care/creation/privacy/export sheets
  portrait. iPad retains native rotation/multitasking. Other platforms need their
  own release validation.
- Fill the age-rating questionnaire for actual capabilities. Do not copy the
  former 4+ assertion or automatically select Kids category; pick an intended
  band only after review. No App Store Connect fields were changed by this work.
- Use the current [child privacy notice](../docs/privacy/protected-play-policy.md)
  and verified data inventory for privacy questionnaire answers. The manifest
  reports no developer collection/tracking; this does not mean private iCloud,
  local processing or parent-directed recipient disclosure is absent.
